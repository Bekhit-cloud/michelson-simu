Michelson Interferometer Simulator

Interactive Optical Bench & Real-Time Wave Interference Lab

1. Optical Bench Schematic (Top View)

Aligned

The laser splits 50/50 at the center beam splitter. One beam reflects off the fixed top mirror (M1), and the other reflects off the movable/tiltable right mirror (M2) before recombining at the detector.

2. Detector Interference Fringes

632.8 nm (He-Ne)

Circular bullseye rings appear when M1 and M2 are parallel. Moving M2 shifts rings inward/outward; tilting M2 turns circular rings into straight parallel fringes.
